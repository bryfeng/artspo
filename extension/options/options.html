Artspo

Your library is the artspo folder in Downloads. The extension keeps a list of what's in it, so it can update a clip instead of duplicating it, and re-inspect it later.

Reconnect a library

Use this after reinstalling Artspo, moving the extension folder, or setting it up on a new computer. Choose your artspo folder. Artspo reads the clip.md files in it and relearns your clips, notes and collections. Nothing in the folder is changed.
